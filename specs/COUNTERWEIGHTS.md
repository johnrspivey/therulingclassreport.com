# Counterweight List — honest voices from the other side

**Purpose:** check RCR's story *choices*, not its conclusions. Run by a right-leaning editor, so this list exists to catch what we'd skip because it doesn't grab us, and to ask "would a fair person on the left call this entry fair?"
**Not for:** settling facts. Facts come from primary records. These voices point at stories; the record decides.
**Started:** Nov 8, 2026. Status: TRIAL — prove the list out before relying on it.

---

## What qualifies someone
Modeled on what John saw in Howard Bloom on election night 2016:
- Openly holds their views (no pretend neutrality)
- Honest in defeat; doesn't win at any cost or defend underhanded tactics
- Criticizes their own side when the record calls for it
- Best fit: cares about money, lobbying, and power — RCR's subject

**Disqualifier:** defending their bias instead of the truth (spins, never corrects, attacks the messenger, goes silent when their side is caught).

## How we use it
- **Weekly (manual for now):** glance at what they're covering. Ask: what are they talking about that RCR skipped? Does it fit RCR's subject (power, money, process)?
- **Per entry (big or partisan stories):** ask Claude/Grok, "How would a fair left-leaning critic read this entry?"
- **Grok:** best tool for watching their X accounts in the meantime.
- **Later automation:** pipeline pulls their headlines as a "stories the other side is talking about" feed (same pattern as Curiosity Check).

## Source credibility record (applies to EVERY source RCR uses, not just this list)
Most people cite sources without checking them. RCR checks.
For each recurring source, keep a short dated record:
- **Track record:** times they got it right vs. wrong on stories RCR followed
- **Corrections:** do they correct openly when wrong? (strongest single trust signal — same standard RCR holds itself to)
- **Own-side criticism:** examples of them holding their own side to account
- **Bias defended vs. truth defended:** dated examples either way
- Status changes are dated updates, never silent edits

## Nod of appreciation (once the list proves sound)
When a source has earned it on the record, RCR gives public credit:
- A "Sources we rely on, and why" page — each source, its lean stated plainly, and the dated evidence it earned trust
- Credit on individual entries where a source led us to a story
- Sources can lose the nod the same way they earned it — on the record, with dates
**Trigger to publish:** several months of dated record entries showing the list works for our use case. Not before.
