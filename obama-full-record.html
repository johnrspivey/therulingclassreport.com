The Obama administration was presented to the American public as the most transparent in history. What the documented record shows is something different — a systematic expansion of executive surveillance, the weaponization of federal agencies against political opponents, and the construction of an intelligence apparatus that would outlast his presidency and be turned against his successor.

01
Domestic Surveillance · NSA · FISA
The Surveillance State — Built and Expanded
NSA Mass Collection of Americans' Phone Records
Documented

The NSA's bulk collection of telephone metadata on American citizens — every call, duration, and number — was revealed by Edward Snowden in 2013. The program operated under Section 215 of the PATRIOT Act. A federal appeals court ruled in 2015 that the program was illegal. Director of National Intelligence James Clapper had told Congress under oath in 2013 that no such collection was occurring. He was not charged with perjury.

Smith-Mundt Modernization Act — Domestic Propaganda Authorized
Documented

In 2012, the Obama administration signed the Smith-Mundt Modernization Act into law as part of the National Defense Authorization Act. The original Smith-Mundt Act of 1948 prohibited the U.S. government from directing propaganda at American citizens. The 2012 amendment lifted that prohibition, allowing State Department and BBG materials produced for foreign audiences to be disseminated domestically. The practical effect was to remove a legal barrier to government-produced media targeting American citizens.

Unmasking of American Citizens in Intelligence Reports
Documented

Declassified documents show that Obama administration officials — including UN Ambassador Samantha Power, National Security Advisor Susan Rice, and CIA Director John Brennan — submitted hundreds of unmasking requests for American citizens in the final year of the Obama administration. Power alone submitted nearly 300 requests. Rice admitted to unmasking in a PBS interview before later denying it. Unmasking itself is legal; the volume and political context drew congressional scrutiny.

Sources: FISA Court opinions (2011, 2015) · Clapper Senate testimony, March 2013 · Smith-Mundt Modernization Act, NDAA 2013, Sec. 1078 · DNI declassified unmasking records (2020) · Rice PBS interview, April 2017
02
IRS · DOJ · Federal Agencies
Weaponization of Federal Agencies
IRS Targeting of Conservative and Tea Party Organizations
Documented

The IRS subjected Tea Party, patriot, and conservative nonprofit applicants to extraordinary scrutiny and delays during the Obama administration — demanding donor lists, social media content, and detailed descriptions of member activities that were not required of comparable progressive organizations. The Treasury Inspector General confirmed the targeting in a 2013 audit. Lois Lerner, head of the IRS Exempt Organizations division, invoked the Fifth Amendment before Congress. The DOJ declined to prosecute. The IRS eventually settled with conservative groups and issued a formal apology.

Operation Fast and Furious
Documented

The Bureau of Alcohol, Tobacco, Firearms and Explosives allowed approximately 2,000 firearms to be sold to straw purchasers with known connections to Mexican drug cartels — with the intent of tracking the weapons to cartel leaders. The weapons were lost. Two of them were recovered at the scene where Border Patrol Agent Brian Terry was murdered in December 2010. Attorney General Eric Holder became the first sitting Cabinet member held in contempt of Congress after refusing to turn over documents. President Obama asserted executive privilege over the subpoenaed materials.

DOJ Surveillance of AP Reporters and James Rosen
Documented

The Obama DOJ secretly obtained two months of phone records from Associated Press reporters and editors in 2012 — more than 20 separate phone lines. Separately, the DOJ named Fox News reporter James Rosen as a criminal co-conspirator in a leak investigation to obtain a warrant for his emails. The AP called it a "massive and unprecedented intrusion." The Committee to Protect Journalists called the Obama administration the most aggressive in U.S. history in prosecuting leakers and surveilling journalists.

Sources: Treasury IG Report — IRS Targeting (2013) · House Oversight Committee — Fast and Furious Report (2012) · Eric Holder contempt citation — Congressional Record (2012) · AP statement on DOJ subpoena (2013) · Committee to Protect Journalists — The Obama Administration and the Press (2013)
03
Iran · Libya · Foreign Policy
Foreign Policy — What Was Hidden
$400 Million Cash Payment to Iran
Documented

In January 2016, the Obama administration transferred $400 million in cash — loaded onto wooden pallets, flown to Tehran on an unmarked cargo plane — to Iran simultaneously with the release of American prisoners. The administration initially denied the payment was ransom. State Department spokesman John Kirby later acknowledged that the cash was used as "leverage" to ensure the prisoners' release. The $400 million was the first installment of a $1.7 billion settlement of a decades-old arms deal dispute. Critics noted the payment emboldened Iranian hardliners and provided hard currency that could fund proxy forces.

Benghazi — The Security Failures and the Cover Story
Documented

On September 11, 2012, Islamic militants attacked the U.S. diplomatic compound and CIA annex in Benghazi, Libya, killing Ambassador Chris Stevens and three other Americans. Multiple requests for additional security prior to the attack had been denied. In the immediate aftermath, the Obama administration — including UN Ambassador Susan Rice on five Sunday news programs — attributed the attack to a spontaneous reaction to an internet video, a characterization contradicted by intelligence available at the time. Emails later revealed that Secretary Clinton told her daughter and the Egyptian prime minister it was a planned terrorist attack on the same night she publicly promoted the video narrative. The Select Committee on Benghazi concluded the administration had misled the public.

"We know that the attack in Libya had nothing to do with the film. It was a planned attack — not a protest."

— Hillary Clinton, in an email to the Egyptian Prime Minister, September 11, 2012 — the same night she publicly attributed the attack to a video
Syrian Red Line — Declared and Abandoned
Documented

In August 2012 President Obama stated publicly that the use of chemical weapons by the Assad regime would cross a "red line" triggering U.S. action. In August 2013, Assad used sarin gas against civilians in Ghouta, killing over 1,400 people. Obama did not act militarily. The administration later claimed the red line had been set by "the world," not Obama personally. The credibility cost — in the Middle East, with European allies, and in the Pacific — was documented by multiple former senior officials in subsequent memoirs and testimony.

Sources: Wall Street Journal — $400M Iran payment reporting (Aug. 2016) · State Dept. briefing, John Kirby (Aug. 18, 2016) · Clinton email to Egyptian PM — released by House Select Committee · House Select Committee on Benghazi Final Report (2016) · Obama "red line" statement — White House press conference, Aug. 20, 2012
04
Crossfire Hurricane · 2016 Election · Intelligence Apparatus
The Intelligence Operation Against a Political Opponent

In the final months of the Obama administration, the apparatus of U.S. intelligence and law enforcement was directed at the campaign of the opposition presidential candidate. What follows is what the formal record — congressional investigations, inspector general reports, and the Durham Special Counsel — established.

Crossfire Hurricane — Opened Without Verified Predicate
Documented

The FBI opened Crossfire Hurricane — a counterintelligence investigation into the Trump campaign — in July 2016. The DOJ Inspector General found the investigation was opened on a "Preliminary Investigation" basis, meaning a lower evidentiary threshold. The Durham Special Counsel Report (2023) found that the FBI had applied a different standard to the Trump investigation than it had to the concurrent Clinton email investigation, and that the predicate for opening was thin by historical FBI standards.

Obama Briefed on Clinton's Russia Collusion Plan
Documented

Declassified CIA Director John Brennan handwritten notes from July 2016 show that Brennan briefed President Obama on intelligence indicating that Hillary Clinton had approved a campaign plan to manufacture a Trump-Russia collusion narrative to distract from her email server scandal. Brennan referred the matter to the FBI. Obama was briefed. No action was taken to stop the investigation that followed.

January 5, 2017 Oval Office Meeting
Documented

Two weeks before Trump's inauguration, President Obama convened a meeting in the Oval Office with Vice President Biden, FBI Director Comey, Deputy AG Sally Yates, CIA Director Brennan, and DNI Clapper — specifically to discuss the Flynn-Kislyak calls and the ongoing Russia investigation. Susan Rice sent herself an email on Inauguration Day documenting that Obama had instructed the group to proceed "by the book." The meeting was later scrutinized as the point at which the incoming administration's National Security Advisor was targeted for removal.

Sources: DOJ IG Report — Crossfire Hurricane (2019) · Durham Special Counsel Report (2023) · Brennan handwritten notes — DNI declassification (Oct. 2020) · Susan Rice email — declassified by Senate Judiciary Committee (2020)

Ideology · Community Organizing · Influence Networks
The Alinsky Foundation — Four Generations Deep

Saul Alinsky's Rules for Radicals (1971) is a tactical manual for community organizing built around the principle that the end justifies the means — that moral standards are tools to be wielded against opponents, not principles to be followed. Obama's political formation ran directly through the Alinsky tradition.

Obama's Direct Training in Alinsky Methods
Documented

Barack Obama worked as a community organizer in Chicago from 1985 to 1988 for the Developing Communities Project — an organization directly descended from Alinsky's Industrial Areas Foundation. He later taught workshops on Alinsky organizing methods. In a 1995 interview, Obama described his organizing work and the Alinsky framework approvingly. His political career was launched from the Hyde Park home of Bill Ayers and Bernardine Dohrn, both former Weather Underground members who had themselves been influenced by the radical left tradition Alinsky helped shape.

Hillary Clinton's Academic Connection
Documented

Hillary Clinton wrote her 1969 senior thesis at Wellesley College on Saul Alinsky's organizing methods — a thesis the Clinton White House later suppressed access to during her husband's presidency. Clinton had corresponded with Alinsky personally and been offered a position with his organization after graduation, which she declined to attend Yale Law School. The thesis was titled "There Is Only the Fight: An Analysis of the Alinsky Model."

Sources: Obama community organizing records — Chicago Tribune reporting (2008) · Obama 1995 interview — Illinois Issues · Clinton Wellesley thesis — released 2001 · Alinsky, Saul — Rules for Radicals (1971)

The Pattern Across Eight Years

What the Obama record shows, assembled in one place, is not a collection of isolated controversies. It is a pattern. The expansion of surveillance powers. The use of federal agencies against political opponents. The construction of a narrative apparatus backed by a modernized legal authority to direct it domestically. And in the final months — the direct application of that apparatus against the incoming administration of the opposing party.

None of the principal actors faced criminal charges. Several became cable news contributors. The institutions they shaped remain in place.
