Our Standard of Evidence

Every single claim on this site carries one of four labels. No exceptions.

Documented

Verified by Primary Sources

Court records, official reports, congressional testimony, or other primary sources confirm the claim.

Alleged

Claimed, Not Yet Proven

Named parties have made the claim, but it has not been confirmed by primary sources or adjudicated.

Disputed

Credible Arguments Both Ways

Credible, sourced arguments exist on both sides. We present both without picking a winner.

Speculative

In Circulation, Unverified

The claim is in public circulation but lacks sourcing sufficient to meet any higher standard.
