How to Get Approved

  1. Confirm you have a qualifying condition Florida covers chronic pain, PTSD, anxiety, cancer, epilepsy, Crohn's, ALS, Parkinson's, HIV, MS, and more. Doctors can also certify conditions "of the same kind or class." Most reputable clinics won't charge you if you don't qualify.
  2. Grab your medical records You don't need a thick file — but prior diagnoses, prescriptions, or test results make approval faster. A year or more of records is ideal.
  3. Book a clinic — in person for first-timers Florida law requires your initial evaluation to be face-to-face. No exceptions for new patients. Telehealth is only for renewals. Compare prices before you commit.
  4. Complete your evaluation Typically 15–30 minutes. The doctor reviews your records, assesses your condition, and if you qualify, enters your info into the state registry — often the same day.
  5. Pay the state fee and apply online After your doctor submits your profile, OMMU emails you to complete your application. Upload proof of Florida residency and pay $77.75. You can shop at dispensaries immediately after doctor approval — before the card arrives.
  6. Receive your card Physical card arrives by mail in 10–15 business days. A digital version comes via email and works at most dispensaries right away.

Common Questions

Can I get my Florida MMJ card fully online?
No — not for new patients. Florida law requires an in-person evaluation for your first certification. Only renewals are eligible for telehealth. Anyone advertising a fully online new-patient process is operating outside state guidelines.
How long does the whole process take?
From booking to shopping at a dispensary: as fast as 1–3 days if your doctor submits same-day. Physical card arrives in 10–15 business days. Your digital card works at dispensaries immediately after state approval.
Does insurance cover any of this?
No. MMJ evaluations and cannabis products are not covered by any insurance in Florida. All costs are out of pocket.
How often do I have to renew?
Your doctor's recommendation must be renewed every 210 days (about 7 months). Your state card is valid for one year. Set a reminder at least 45 days before expiration.
What if I don't qualify?
Most reputable clinics — including MMJ Health, DocMJ, and My Florida Green — offer a full refund if the physician determines you don't qualify. Confirm this policy before you book.
Can I use my card in other states?
No. Florida MMJ cards are only valid in Florida. Florida does not currently participate in any cross-state MMJ recognition agreements.
