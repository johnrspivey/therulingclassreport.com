Our Standard
Four labels. No exceptions.

Every claim on this platform carries one of four labels before you read a word of it.

Documented
Verified by primary sources

Court records, official findings, signed laws, congressional reports, verified documents. We cite the source. You can check it yourself.

Alleged
Claimed, not conclusively proven

A named person or organization made this claim on record. It has not been confirmed by independent evidence. We tell you who said it and why it matters.

Disputed
Credible arguments both ways

Reasonable, informed people examining the same evidence reach different conclusions. We show both sides. We do not pick a winner on genuinely contested questions.

Speculative
In circulation, unverified

This claim exists publicly. We present it with context, explain what would verify it, and update the label if evidence emerges. We never present speculation as fact.
