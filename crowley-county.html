Crowley County, Colorado

The Water Rights Theft That Destroyed a Community

The Story

In the 1970s and 1980s, water rights were systematically stripped from farmers and ranchers in Crowley County and transferred to the growing Denver metro area. This was not an act of nature — it was a deliberate political and bureaucratic decision.

The result was the economic collapse of a once-thriving agricultural region. Families lost land that had been in their possession for generations.

Key Facts

  • Crowley County population dropped dramatically after water transfers
  • Local economy shifted from prosperous agriculture to poverty
  • This pattern of rural water reallocation has repeated across the American West
