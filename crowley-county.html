Crowley County, Colorado

The Systematic Theft of Water Rights and the Death of a Rural Community

The Record

In the 1970s and 1980s, Colorado political leaders and water authorities orchestrated the transfer of water rights from family farms and ranches in Crowley County to the booming Denver metropolitan area. This was done through legal and bureaucratic maneuvers that prioritized urban growth over longstanding rural property rights.

Consequences

  • Severe population decline as families lost their land and livelihood
  • Economic collapse of what was once a prosperous agricultural region
  • Loss of multi-generational family heritage and community infrastructure
  • Creation of a precedent for similar water reallocations across the rural West
