Crowley County, Colorado

Water Rights and Rural Community Change

Historical Background

During the 1970s and 1980s, significant water rights transfers occurred in the Arkansas River Basin. Many water rights previously used for agriculture in Crowley County were acquired by municipalities to support growing urban needs in the Denver area.

Impact on the Community

These changes contributed to economic challenges for the region. Crowley County, once known for its productive farmland, experienced population decline and difficulties maintaining its agricultural economy.

Significance

This case illustrates the complex tensions between rural agricultural communities and urban growth in the American West.
